# Rulebook Errata (v0.8.2.4)

Places where the rules docx disagrees with the owner's rulings in
[DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md). The TTS mod follows DECISIONS.md. This list is
for the owner to fold into the tabletop rulebook; this project does not
rewrite the rules.

| Rulebook section | Text says | Ruling | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wind Phase > Drift | Wrecks move 2" with the wind | Defeated ships drift 1.5"; wrecks are removed | D-006 |
| Movement | A ship that elects not to move drifts 2" | 1.5" | D-017 |
| Movement | Backward moves have a flat 1/4 movement (turning unstated) | Backward moves may turn | D-016 |
| Movement | Land: "front edge ... or a portion of the base ending the movement in contact" | Leading edge (rear edge when backward) may not touch land; the rest of the base may sweep over it; no overlap at the end | D-020 |
| Health > Durability | Target number depends on shot type and range | D20 + durability modifier ≥ shot damage negates the hit | D-001 |
| Shooting table | "Tail On +3 mod." | −3 tail-on durability modifier | D-004 |
| Shooting | Tail-on mentions only the durability penalty | Tail-on is also −2 to hit, like head-on | D-003 |
| Health > Armor | The rear of a ship has no armor | Every hit lands on left or right armor | D-002 |
| Faction tables | Atrytian and Denrudain 5th Rate crew 3 | 4 | D-013 |
| Faction tables | Denrudain 3rd Rate cargo 4 | 5 | D-014 |
| Wind Shift | Mild shift size unstated; no-wind movement unstated | One D6 point; no wind means 1x for all ships | D-009, D-010 |
| Secret objective "From Behind" | Hull damage through a shot to the rear arc | With D-002 that side's armor must be depleted first. **Owner to confirm this is intended.** | D-002 |
